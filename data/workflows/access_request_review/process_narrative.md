# Process Narrative

The access request review workflow manages employee requests for application, data, and system access.

Managers submit access requests for employees who need new or changed permissions. The identity team reviews required intake fields, verifies employee information against the HR system, and checks whether the request involves privileged access, sensitive systems, custom roles, APIs, or security-related permissions.

Requests with missing, unclear, or conflicting information are routed back for clarification. Requests involving privileged access, sensitive systems, or elevated permissions require application owner and security review before access can be provisioned.

After approval, IT provisions access in the identity provider and target systems. The ticket status is updated, evidence is recorded, and unresolved requests near the SLA deadline are escalated.

The workflow is a candidate for AI assistance in intake review, evidence gathering, draft recommendations, and backlog planning, but provisioning actions and access decisions require human approval and auditability.
