# Policy and Controls

- Access requests require a valid employee identifier, manager, requested system, access level, and business justification.
- Requests with missing, unclear, incomplete, or conflicting intake information must be routed back for clarification.
- Privileged access requires application owner approval and security review.
- Access to sensitive systems requires additional review before provisioning.
- Provisioning actions must not occur until required approvals are recorded.
- Ticket status updates must preserve approval evidence and reviewer information.
- SLA deadline escalations must be visible to the identity team lead.
- Audit evidence must be retained for access approvals, provisioning actions, and rejected requests.
- Write actions require explicit human approval before execution.
